The complementary twist reads as a hand-off between two side-by-side bands, with A wide and bright on the left at the top, B the wide one through the middle while A thins to a near-invisible thread at y of about 650, and A wide again at the bottom, but the study's crossing never happens: with a fixed ±70 lateral offset the two bands share zero pixels and only come edge to edge (1 px apart at y of about 200 to 300, 13 px at y of about 850 to 950).
Letting the offset drift (dx from ∓70 to ±70, so the centre lines meet at t = 0.5) did not fix it, because the bands then overlapped on 1306 of 1400 rows by 12 to 105 px, which brought back the full-page mesh, and the "thread through a band" moment at y of about 600 to 650 is a 12 to 15 px strip at alpha of about 0.12 that the fade makes almost invisible; that render was rejected, and this is the fixed-offset one.
At 360 px the two bands and their swap read, with a dark channel between them in the lower half, but at the top they abut and read as one band, half bright and half grey, and since the renderer max-merges lines with no occlusion, the ±70 depth offset can never show one ribbon passing in front of the other, so this study fails as a crossing.
